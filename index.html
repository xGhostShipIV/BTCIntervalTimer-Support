Quick fixes

  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Confirm your device volume is turned up and Silent Mode is off if you cannot hear timer sounds.
  • Install the latest available version of BTC Interval Timer and iOS.
  • Restart your device if the problem continues.

Helpful details

When contacting support, include:

  • Your device model and iOS version.
  • The app version shown on your device.
  • The steps that led to the problem.
  • A screenshot, if it helps and contains no sensitive information.

Your privacy

Saved timers and recent timer usage remain on your device. The app also uses Google Mobile Ads, which may process information to provide and measure advertising.
